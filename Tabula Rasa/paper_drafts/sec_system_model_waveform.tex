\section{System Model}
\label{sec:system}

We consider a single-carrier link on which a legitimate node transmits QPSK to a second legitimate node; the receiving node monitors the link for interference.
A team $\mathcal{J}=\{1,\ldots,N_J\}$ of jammers seeks to raise the receiver's error rate while remaining below its detector.
The transmitter, receiver, and detector form one fixed target: only the jammer team is optimized, against a static detection rule and a fixed demodulation chain.
% future work, accurate scope statements -- keep both.
\rar{Legitimate nodes take fixed TX/RX roles; symmetric transceivers, as in a multi-node network, are future work.}
\rar{A co-adaptive defender is future work; the fixed-defender setting isolates the jammer-side problem, and adaptation is treated as round-based and offline.}

\subsection{Legitimate Link}
\label{sec:link}
% Single carrier -- NOT OFDM. cgan/link.py. This is the core correction to the
% Overleaf draft.
The transmitter sends frames of $N$ unit-energy QPSK symbols $s[k]\in\mathcal{Q}=\{\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(\pm1\pm j)\}$, drawn independently and uniformly.
% i.i.d. uniform matters: a scrambled payload has no structure to exploit, so the
% single-link minimum-energy attack is closed form (README Sec. 2.5, 4.2 Q2).
Each frame is upsampled by $L$ samples per symbol and shaped by a unit-energy root-raised-cosine pulse $g$ with roll-off $\beta$, giving the transmit waveform
\begin{equation}
x[n]=\sum_{k=1}^{N} s[k]\,g[n-kL].
\label{eq:tx}
\end{equation}
The receiver applies the matched filter $g$, samples at the symbol instants, and makes a per-axis hard decision; the pulse is root-Nyquist, so the cascade is inter-symbol-interference-free up to filter truncation.
% span 32 (not 8): truncation ISI 3.6% -> 0.3%, else it flips zero-margin symbols
% the paper's model does not have (cgan/link.py RRC_SPAN; verify job 2259141).
Bit and symbol error rate under this chain are the effectiveness metrics.
Bit-error recovery is out of scope: we assume forward error correction and retransmission are handled by a higher layer, and report the raw error rate because it is the input that layer receives, and because pushing it past the code's correcting capability is what becomes outage.
% FEC-out is load-bearing for any stealth claim (README Sec. 2.9): state it as a
% deliberate assumption, not a convenience.

\subsection{Channel Model and Received Signal}
\label{sec:channel}
% LOS + power control, NO fading, NO ZF. cgan/scene.py, cgan/channel.py.
Nodes are placed in a three-dimensional region and the transmitter--receiver and jammer--receiver links are line-of-sight, so each is a single path with a free-space power gain $g_{iR}=(\lambda/4\pi d_{iR})^2$ obtained by ray tracing~\cite{sionna}.
The received frame is the composite
\begin{equation}
r[n]=x[n]+\sum_{j\in\mathcal{J}} c_j[n]+w[n],\qquad w[n]\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,N_0),
\label{eq:rx}
\end{equation}
where $c_j$ is jammer $j$'s waveform as it arrives at the receiver and $N_0$ fixes the signal-to-noise ratio.
The transmitter applies open-loop power control with full path-loss compensation to a target $\mathrm{SNR}$ at the receiver, so the clean received law is identical in every geometry and one detector calibration (Sec.~\ref{sec:detector}) serves them all.
% This is why there is no per-drop retraining and no equalization term.
SNR and jamming-to-signal ratio (JSR) are defined as mean power per sample at the receiver input over the simulated band; a single jammer is then fully described at the receiver by its received $\mathrm{JSR}_j=P_j\,g_{jR}/P_{\mathrm{rx}}$, with $P_j$ its transmit power and $P_{\mathrm{rx}}$ the controlled received signal power.
% white noise is suppressed by the full processing gain L at the matched filter;
% a matched-modulation jammer is not -- this spread is what the ladder spans.
Every realizable jammer is asynchronous: its timing offset is uniform over one symbol and its carrier phase uniform over $[0,2\pi)$, drawn per frame, and a geometric delay or phase adds nothing in law on top of these.
% checked in cgan/verify.py, not assumed (README Sec. 3.3d): on/off geometric
% delay+phase give the same BER and P(det). Only the genie (T0) is synchronous.
A single line-of-sight, power-controlled link needs no equalization, so geometry enters the model only through $\mathrm{JSR}_j$ and this asynchronism.
For $N_J\!\ge\!2$, jammers split a total budget equally and their independent contributions sum at the receiver; a phase-aligned team that concentrates the split on the strongest links is the coherent genie reference of Sec.~\ref{sec:attacker}.
% uncoordinated K>1 buys nothing at matched detectability (README Sec. 3.3c #4,
% 3.3d) -- it is the baseline any coordination result must beat, not a result.

\subsection{Attacker Model}
\label{sec:attacker}
% Was "Cooperative Jammer Team". Renamed: coordination is future/gated, so the
% old header over-promised. cgan/attacks.py, jammers.py, train_shaped.py.
Every attacker, closed-form or learned, is a conditional law $c_j\sim\pi_j(\cdot\mid\xi_j)$, where $\xi_j$ is the information available when it forms its signal; attacks differ only in $\xi_j$ and in the family $\pi_j$ ranges over.
% the central abstraction: it puts closed-form and learned attacks on one axis.
The power budget is a hard constraint: each transmitted frame is projected onto its target JSR,
\begin{equation}
\frac{1}{N L}\sum_{n}|c_j[n]|^2 \;=\; \mathrm{JSR}_j\,P_{\mathrm{rx}},
\label{eq:power}
\end{equation}
so no attacker can trade effectiveness against a soft power penalty.
% equality here (the sweep imposes a JSR); the <= budget form is for a learned
% jammer that may choose to spend less. Never a loss term (README Sec. 2.8).
Three knowledge tiers define $\xi_j$ and results are reported per tier: a genie (T0) that knows the current symbol and is synchronous; a realistic attacker (T1) that is asynchronous and has only link statistics; and a blind attacker (T2) that has neither.
Five strategies instantiate $\pi_j$ and every one appears in every results figure, so each curve is read between a floor and a ceiling.

\begin{table}[t]\centering\small

\label{tab:ladder}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}

The pulsed jammer transmits the victim's own modulation at duty cycle $p$; $p{=}1$ is the matched-modulation optimum of~\cite{zhou2025cgan} and $p{<}1$ the pulsed family that is BER-optimal at low JSR~\cite{amuru2015optimal}.
The shaped and GAN strategies are the learned tiers, optimized against the detector's own decisions under the objective of Sec.~\ref{sec:objective}: the shaped jammer over a low-dimensional spectral-and-envelope parameter $\theta$, the generator over its output samples through a differentiable critic.
% shaped vs. GAN is the ablation that isolates the hypothesis class (low-dim
% params vs. raw IQ) from the objective -- both carry the same reward + detectors.
The omniscient strategy needs the current symbol and a receiver-synchronous arrival, so it is unrealizable and serves only as an upper bound: at $\eta{=}1$ it inverts attacked symbols, leaving the received law unchanged from clean, and at $\eta{<}1$ it pushes just past the decision boundary, which lowers received power.
% eta=1: received law == clean => undetectable by ANY test, BER = delta.
% eta<1: power drops => blind to the one-sided energy test (the classical
% blind spot). delta = min(1, JSR/(1+eta)^2) (cgan/attacks.omniscient_rx).

\subsection{Detector Model}
\label{sec:detector}
% Four SEPARATE detectors + NP ceiling. NOT an energy-OR-CNN suite (that was the
% frozen sim08 stack). cgan/detectors.py, baselines.py.
Detection is at the receiver, on one received frame $r$, with no channel state information and no run-time labels.
The defender is a bank of detectors, each reduced to a scalar statistic built so that larger is more suspicious and each thresholded at its own false-alarm rate $\alpha$, set from the empirical $(1{-}\alpha)$ quantile of clean frames so the rate holds by construction.
They are reported separately, never combined, so that a per-detector blind spot stays visible.
% deliberate change from the OR-suite: OR-combining hides which detector fails.
An energy detector thresholds mean frame power, in a one-sided form, blind to power-reducing attacks, and a two-sided form~\cite{urkowitz1967energy}.
A kurtosis detector thresholds $\mathbb{E}|r|^4/(\mathbb{E}|r|^2)^2$, which is scale-free and sees the amplitude shape that energy cannot.
A learned detector applies a convolutional network to the two-sided complex spectrogram of $r$ on a fixed decibel scale, the single-carrier counterpart of the spectrogram classifiers that define the state of the art~\cite{li2022jamming_ofdm}.
% fixed dB scale (not per-image contrast stretch): the stretch erases a jammer
% that lifts the whole band evenly (README Sec. 3.3d, A.4 refinement).
Finally, the Neyman--Pearson likelihood-ratio test for a white-Gaussian jammer of known received power is included as the optimal reference; the single-carrier model makes it available in closed form from the matched-filter samples and the residual out-of-band energy.
% NP applies exactly to the analytic (noise/shaped) rows; the GAN's density needs
% a KDE. This is the whole reason to reduce to single carrier.
This reference is what the model is reduced for: it turns every claim of the form ``the trained detector misses this'' into ``no detector does better'', and the gap between it and the deployed detectors is a measured quantity.
By construction $P_{\mathrm{det}}^{\mathrm{NP}}\ge P_{\mathrm{det}}^{\mathrm{CNN}}\ge P_{\mathrm{det}}^{\mathrm{energy}}$.

\subsection{Threat Model}
\label{sec:threat}
% Kept from the old draft with light edits (scalar -> per-frame score).
A learned jammer observes each detector only through its per-frame score at the calibrated threshold; detector weights, gradients, and architecture are not observed, and the detector is frozen, so any adaptation is round-based and offline.
\rar{A grey-box surrogate-gradient transfer attack is a possible relaxation, future work.}
A flagged jammer forfeits future effectiveness --- localization, frequency reallocation, or spatial nulling neutralize it --- which is what motivates the detection constraint below.
Physical-layer localization is itself a defender capability but is out of scope, since it needs distributed infrastructure the baseband model does not represent.

\subsection{Problem Formulation}
\label{sec:objective}
The jammer team maximizes the induced error rate penalized by detection,
\begin{equation}
\max_{\{\pi_j\}}\;\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{BER}]-\beta\,P_{\mathrm{det}}(\alpha)\quad\text{s.t. Eq.~\eqref{eq:power}},
\label{eq:problem}
\end{equation}
with no further terms, and results are read as the achievable frontier $\mathrm{BER}^\star(\beta)=\max\{\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{BER}]:P_{\mathrm{det}}\le\beta\}$ for each detector, equivalent to the constrained form $\max\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{BER}]$ subject to $P_{\mathrm{det}}\le\varepsilon$.
% Lagrangian form is what the code optimizes; the constrained/frontier form is
% what is reported. State both so a reviewer sees no mismatch.
Two reporting conventions are fixed.
Strategies are compared at matched detectability rather than at matched power or configuration, since an attack that raises the error rate by raising its own signature is not an improvement.
% the project already lost a "+70%" headline to matched-config vs matched-P(det)
% (README Sec. 2.7).
The stealth budget is the detector's own clean false-alarm rate, not a loose threshold such as $P_{\mathrm{det}}\le\tfrac12$, because a jammer caught in half of every frame is caught within a few frames.
We therefore report the full BER-versus-$P_{\mathrm{det}}$ frontier over all budgets, with the clean false-alarm rate marked, alongside the dual-axis view of error rate and detection probability against the swept parameter, with the no-attacker floor and the omniscient ceiling drawn in.
% never quote only the value at alpha (memory: show-full-frontier). Report the
% measured effect size at matched BER, not just the bound (memory:
% report-measured-gain-not-impossibility).
Whether any realizable strategy reaches a jointly effective-and-undetected regime, and how far the deployed detectors fall short of the optimal test, is the paper's central question.
