\subsection{Summary of Hypotheses and Verdicts}
\label{subsec:exphist:verdicts}
Table~\ref{tab:verdicts} collects the questions each step was built to answer.
Four entries are verdicts against claims we had ourselves reported, and each names the later
measurement that overturned it; those rows are the reason the reporting rules the results sections
follow are what they are.

\begin{table*}[!t]
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\caption{What each step asked, what answered it, and what the answer was.}
\label{tab:verdicts}
\centering
\begin{tabular}{p{0.30\textwidth} p{0.30\textwidth} l p{0.20\textwidth}}
\hline\hline
\textbf{Hypothesis} & \textbf{Evidence} & \textbf{Verdict} & \textbf{Superseded by} \\
\hline
A power threshold is evaded by power tuning alone
& BER $0.19$ at $10\,\%$ detection, power held just below the threshold
& supported & --- \\

A diagonal Gaussian policy can produce QPSK-like received statistics
& detection at $100\,\%$, received kurtosis stalled at $-0.25$
& refuted, structurally & --- \\

A more expressive action distribution repairs it under policy gradient
& flow and mixture heads stall at the same kurtosis; nine mixture runs, one of $10^6$ steps
& refuted & --- \\

The same flow trained by direct gradient evades the kurtosis test
& kurtosis $-1.31$ at BER $0.170$, detection $\approx 0\,\%$
& supported, under genie observation
& black-box observation, \S\ref{subsec:exphist:untrainable} \\

Two coordinated agents outperform one at equal total power
& BER $0.195$ against $0.170$ at total power $\approx 0.97$
& supported, by $\approx 15\,\%$
& retracts our ``roughly double'' \\

The larger two-agent gain is coordination rather than power
& $3.2\times$ the power for $2.7\times$ the BER; the power term never binds
& refuted for that operating point
& hard power budget \\

A published spectrogram classifier reproduces on our chain
& $78.9\,\%$ on a flat carrier, $99.79\,\%$ on OFDM, waveform the only variable
& supported only with OFDM & --- \\

Policy-gradient learning over raw IQ yields a stealthy jammer
& detector output pinned at $0.999$ across every run; entropy flat to $0.05\,\%$
& refuted, structurally
& low-dimensional perturbation parameters \\

Effectiveness and stealth trade off fundamentally, capping BER near $0.04$
& the cap was guard-bin occupancy, not a detector property
& refuted
& characterisation sweep, \S\ref{subsec:exphist:characterisation} \\

The classifier's $99.79\,\%$ measures resistance to jamming
& flags power $0.03$ out-of-band causing no errors; misses in-band interference at BER $0.36$
& refuted & --- \\

The in-band blind spot is a gap in the training data
& retraining closes it at accuracy $99.8 \to 90.5\,\%$, false alarms $0 \to 3.8\,\%$
& refuted; it is a trade-off & --- \\

The real-part-only spectrogram invalidates the out-of-band finding
& complex transform: in-band $0.086$ against out-of-band $1.000$ at equal damage
& refuted; the finding stands & --- \\

A stealthy attacker reaching BER $0.42$ exists on the lossless channel
& against the suite, at most $0.005$ at any usable budget
& refuted
& energy detector, \S\ref{subsec:exphist:characterisation} \\

A sparse in-band jammer imposes a BER floor independent of signal-to-noise ratio
& $0.05$--$0.07$ at eight subcarriers across 5--30\,dB
& supported & --- \\

The energy detector is redundant beside the classifier
& lossless: the meter subsumes the classifier; faded: the classifier subsumes the meter
& conditional on the channel & --- \\

Channel-aware subcarrier selection beats blind selection
& $+70\,\%$ at matched configuration; $-4.6$ to $+6.2\,\%$ at matched detectability
& refuted at matched detectability
& dense sweep, \S\ref{subsec:exphist:realistic} \\

An effective and unflagged attacker exists on a realistic channel
& at the defender's own false-alarm rate: none at 5--15\,dB, BER $0.011$ and $0.004$ at 20 and 30\,dB
& supported, and modest
& retracts our $\varepsilon \leq 0.5$ figure \\
\hline\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table*}
